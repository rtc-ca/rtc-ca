Help Center

How to Use

  • 1. Fill Header — Enter Date, Client, Project, Ref No., and Quotation Number at the top.
  • 2. Add Items — Click + Add Row or type the number of rows and click Create Rows.
  • 3. Fill Each Row — Enter Item/Supplier name, upload an image, fill Cost and PDM. Selling and Qty are required for computation.
  • 4. Adjust Markup — Default is 40%. Change the % per row if needed.
  • 5. Check Summary — Summary panel on the right auto-updates as you type.
  • 6. Add Breakdowns — Optional Goods Breakdown and PDM/Customized Breakdown at the bottom.
  • 7. Sign — Fill in signatory names at the bottom.
  • 8. Export — Click Export PDF to save or print.
  • 9. Multiple Quotes — Use tabs at the top. Click ＋ New Quote to add. Double-click tab name to rename.
  • 10. Auto-Save — Data saves automatically every 2 seconds to your browser.

Image per Row

  • Click the image cell — focuses it (enables Ctrl+V paste)
  • Insert button — opens file picker to upload from your device
  • Ctrl+V — focus the cell first, then paste a copied image
  • Right-click — shows menu: Paste Image, Upload Image, Remove Image
  • Backspace / Delete key — focus the cell then press to remove image
  • Replace — just Insert or paste a new image over the existing one
  • Images auto-fit the cell — portrait, landscape, or square — no distortion

Field Definitions

CostBase cost of the item from supplier.
PDMAdditional cost (printing, delivery, miscellaneous).
Total CostCost + PDM. Auto-computed.
MarkupTotal Cost × (1 + Markup%). Default 40%. Editable per row.
SellingYour selling price per unit. You enter this.
QtyNumber of units.
Total SellingSelling × Qty. Auto-computed.
Total Cost AmtTotal Cost × Qty. Auto-computed.
ProfitTotal Selling − Total Cost Amt. Auto-computed.
Profit %Profit ÷ Total Selling × 100. Auto-computed.
Gross MarginTotal Profit ÷ Total Selling × 100.
Net MarginGross Margin after 10% tax deduction.
EWTExpanded Withholding Tax. Optional, assumed by RTC.

Net Margin Guide

Minimum target: 20%

Net Margin = Gross Margin − 10% (tax). If your gross margin is 30%, net margin is 20% — the minimum acceptable.

🔴 TOO LOW (below 20%) — selling price is too low relative to cost. You need to either raise the selling price or reduce the cost.

🟢 GOOD (20% and above) — acceptable margin. The higher, the better.

How to fix a low margin:

  • Increase the Selling price per unit
  • Reduce Cost or PDM if possible
  • Check if Markup % is set correctly per item
  • Review Other Expenses % in the summary

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my Net Margin red?
It's below 20%. Increase your selling price or reduce costs.

How do I rename a tab?
Double-click the tab name and type the new name. Press Enter to confirm.

Is my data saved automatically?
Yes — every 2 seconds, your data is saved to your browser's local storage.

Can others see my saved data?
No. Data is stored only in your browser on your device. It is private.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?
Enter — move to next row
Shift + Enter — new line in Item/Supplier field
Tab — move to next cell

Need help or suggestions?
Contact Executive Assistant Department or Isaac.

How do I install this as an app?

Option 1: Click the 📲 Install App button in the header (if visible). Click Install when prompted. Once installed, you can open it like a regular app on your phone or desktop. The button disappears after installation.

Option 2: On Google Chrome, click the three dots (⋮) on the top right corner of the browser. In the menu, click "Cast, save, and share". Then click "Install page as app". A dialog will appear — click "Install" or "Done". The app will be added to your desktop or home screen.

Settings Guide

How to open Settings
Click the ⚙ Settings button in the header.

Font Style
Choose between Arial, DM Sans, or Calibri. Changes apply immediately to all text on screen.

Font Size
Drag the slider between 10px and 15px. Default is 12px. Font size has no effect on PDF export.

Bold Text
Toggle bold on or off. Bold is included in PDF export.

Reset to Default
Resets font to Arial, size to 12px, bold off.

Signatory Names
Save names and positions for Prepared by and Noted by.

  • Type a name and position, then click + Add Name
  • First in the list = DEFAULT — auto-fills all tabs including new ones
  • Click ↑ to move a name up (make it default)
  • Click ✕ to delete a name
  • In the signature section, type a name to see dropdown suggestions
  • Click a suggestion to auto-fill name and position
